How can I check medicine availability?+

Use the medicine enquiry form or WhatsApp us with the medicine name and quantity. Our team will confirm availability.

Can I send a prescription on WhatsApp?+

Yes, you can send your enquiry first. If a prescription is needed, attach a clear photo or PDF directly in WhatsApp after it opens.

How can I book a physiotherapy appointment?+

Complete the appointment enquiry form. It opens WhatsApp with your details so the clinic can confirm a suitable time.

What physiotherapy services are available?+

We can discuss back or neck discomfort, knee and joint care, muscle stiffness, sports injury rehabilitation, mobility, flexibility and rehabilitation needs.

Where is Patel Medical Store located?+

On Taraya Road, Tamkuhi Raj, opposite Shagun Marriage Hall. Use the directions link on this page to open Google Maps.

Do I need to confirm my appointment before visiting?+

Yes. Appointment requests are not confirmed until the clinic replies and confirms the time.
